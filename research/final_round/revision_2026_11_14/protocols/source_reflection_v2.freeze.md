# PUL-source exact-matched geometry reflection, mechanism follow-up v2

Created 2026-11-14 after the first source-space experiment exposed severe unequal-energy feasibility for naive random source directions. No v2 neural response outcomes were inspected at freeze. This is a declared protocol amendment, not an independent preregistered test.

Use the same fixed nine original networks and same 12 paired test base histories (seed 94002) crossed with original strong fifth stimuli 2 and 18 positive pips. For each 5-ms input point, use natural PUL pair mean mu and difference d. Let cap_i=2min(mu_i,20-mu_i), B=W_PPC,PUL, M=B^T B. Let A be the 2x70 matrix giving summed PUL input to the *actual native* PPC E and I classes. Construct candidate tangent vectors z satisfying A z=0 and z_i=0 where natural d has no slack. For each candidate, define alpha=-2(d^T M z)/(z^T M z), d'=d+alpha z. This exactly preserves ||Bd'||^2=||Bd||^2 and E/I sums A d'=A d. Keep candidates satisfying |d'_i|<=cap_i. Among 32 fixed-seed candidates, select the one with the largest receiver energy-norm difference ||B(d'-d)||/||B d||, purely from source geometry. Two independent maps per checkpoint; zero-change fallback is allowed, reported, and never selected by behavior. Both source branch vectors mu +/- d'/2 remain in [0,20], and the *entire 70-D pair mean* remains mu.

Experimental conditions: natural intact, allstate mean+own natural, allstate mean+pair-mean, allstate mean+mapped source (2 maps), allstate mean+opposite. The 1s intervention is [1400,1600), then free run. 80-step current and historical windows are [1720,1800) and [2120,2200). Every condition is tested on all contexts, no trial or network exclusion for feasibility. Report feasible fraction, achieved angular/current reallocation, exact invariants and native current success. Test hypothesis: within physically source-reachable exact aggregate/energy constraints, does the learned historical source allocation produce a larger future historical contrast than alternative allocations?

Important: This is a source-edge intervention in a fixed model, not an optogenetic manipulation or proof that the PUL recurrent dynamics can autonomously generate every imposed trajectory. The geometry perturbation may be small when the native source state is close to its positivity boundary; negative effects then quantify feasible local rigidity rather than evidence of one unique code.
